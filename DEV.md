# **Dev commands:**  
These are extra commands that make bigger changes beyond the simple ones listed in github. All commands will start with `dev`  

## Wipe:
*Usage: `dev wipe`*  
Resets the stock amount of all items in all categories to 0.
## Wipe Category:
*Usage: `dev wipe category [category_name]`*  
Resets the stock amount of all items in category to 0.
